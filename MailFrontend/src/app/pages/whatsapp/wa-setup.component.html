WhatsApp campaigns go through Meta's official WhatsApp Business Platform — the only way to message many people without the number being banned. You need:

  • 1 A mobile number that is not on WhatsApp right now. A new SIM is easiest. A number already on the WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business app must be deleted from the app first — it cannot be in both places.
  • 2 A Facebook login to create a free Meta business account and app. No Facebook account? Create one at facebook.com with your real name — it is only for logging in to Meta's business tools, nothing gets posted. It is needed for this setup; after it, templates, campaigns and replies all work from this panel.
  • 3 A payment card, added later in WhatsApp Manager. Meta charges a small fee per marketing message; replies within 24 hours are free.

Just trying it? Skip the number for now: Meta gives every new app a free test number that can message up to five phones you add on the API Setup page.

  1. Open developers.facebook.com → Log in (top right) with your Facebook account → My Apps. The first time, Meta asks you to Get started as a developer: accept and confirm your phone or email, once. Meta says "the link you followed may be broken"? You are not logged in yet — press Login at the top right.
  2. Create app → choose the use case "Connect with customers through WhatsApp" (or type Business) → give it a name like "Dharstec WhatsApp" → pick or create your business portfolio.
  3. In the app, open WhatsApp → API Setup. Add your number under "Add phone number" and confirm the code Meta sends — or keep the test number for now.

The token on the API Setup page stops working after 24 hours. Make one that never expires:

  1. Open business.facebook.com → Settings → Users → System users → Add → name it "CommonMailer", role Admin.
  2. Assign assets: your app with Full control, and your WhatsApp account with Full control.
  3. Generate new token → choose the app → expiry Never → tick whatsapp_business_messaging and whatsapp_business_management → Generate → copy it.
