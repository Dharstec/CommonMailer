Getting into the inbox

  • Send from the customer's own domain, with SPF, DKIM and DMARC set up at the domain host.
  • A new mailbox? Start with 30–50 a day and grow it over two weeks.
  • Personalise the subject and greeting; avoid ALL CAPS and "FREE!!!".
  • The Personal design reaches Gmail's Primary tab most often.
  • Email people who would expect to hear from you — complaints hurt more than anything else.
